Disclaimer

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Veridian Global Advisors ("Veridian," "we," "us," or "our") is a private advisory firm that provides document preparation and case-organization services to individuals pursuing U.S. residency. Please read this disclaimer carefully before engaging our services.

Not Affiliated With Any Government Agency

Veridian Global Advisors is an independent, privately owned company. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the U.S. Department of State, or any other government agency, in the United States or elsewhere. Any government forms, terminology, or requirements referenced on this site are described for informational purposes only.

Not a Law Firm

Veridian Global Advisors is not a law firm and does not employ or hold itself out as employing licensed attorneys acting in that capacity on behalf of clients. We do not provide legal advice, do not offer legal opinions on the merits of any case, and do not represent clients before USCIS, the Department of State, any court, or any other government agency. No attorney-client relationship is formed by using our services, visiting this website, or communicating with our team. Clients are required to retain their own independent, licensed immigration attorney for legal advice, case strategy, and filing.

No Guarantee of Approval

Immigration outcomes are decided solely by government agencies, based on criteria and processes outside of Veridian's control. Nothing on this website, in our marketing materials, or in any communication from our team constitutes a promise, warranty, or guarantee that any application, petition, or filing will be approved, or that it will be processed within any particular timeframe by the relevant government agency. References to "speed" or "timeline" on this site describe our internal document-preparation process only, not government processing time.

Refund Policy

Engagement fees are structured as a non-refundable retainer, described further in our Terms of Service, in exchange for the immediate allocation of advisor and case-manager time to your file.

The sole exception is our Executive tier completion guarantee: if we exceed 28 calendar days to complete document preparation due to an error or delay on our part, you receive a $12,500 refund. This guarantee does not apply to the Foundation or Accelerated tiers, and does not cover delays caused by government processing times, incomplete or late client documentation, or other circumstances outside our reasonable control. Full guarantee terms are reviewed and confirmed in writing during your consultation before any retainer is signed.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Veridian Global Advisors collects through this website and our confidential review process, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

What We Collect

When you submit our confidential review form, we collect the information you provide directly:

  • Full name
  • Country of residence
  • Declared net worth range
  • Email address
  • Service tier of interest and any notes you choose to share

If you proceed to an engagement, we collect the additional financial and identity documentation necessary to prepare your file (for example, tax records, bank statements, and source-of-funds documentation), as described in our AML Statement. We also automatically receive standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) when you visit this website, for security and site-operation purposes.

How We Use It

  • Client communication — to respond to your inquiry, schedule your consultation, and communicate with you throughout an engagement.
  • Service delivery — to prepare, organize, and quality-check your documentation.
  • Compliance — to meet our anti-money-laundering, source-of-funds, and recordkeeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or targeted marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

We share limited information with a small number of service providers strictly as needed to operate our business, including our email delivery provider (to send you confirmation and follow-up correspondence) and our internal case-management system (to track your engagement). These providers are contractually restricted from using your information for any purpose other than providing services to us. We do not share your information with unrelated third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion of your information, subject to our legal recordkeeping and AML obligations described below. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@veridianglobal.com.

Data Retention

We retain personal information collected through this website and during an engagement for two (2) years from the date of your last interaction with us, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law or our AML compliance obligations, after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information should be directed to hello@veridianglobal.com.
