Questions

Frequently Asked

What happens if my case takes longer than the quoted timeline?

For Executive engagements, our 28-day guarantee means that if the delay is due to our error, you receive a $12,500 refund. Delays caused by government processing times or missing client documentation aren't covered — your advisor will flag those risks upfront during your confidential review.

Do you provide legal advice or represent me before USCIS?

No. Veridian Global Advisors is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not represent clients before any government agency. We prepare and organize your documentation; your independent, licensed immigration attorney handles filing and legal strategy.
