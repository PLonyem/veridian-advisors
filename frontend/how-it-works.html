Transparency

What We Don't Do

We'd rather be upfront about the limits of what we control than overpromise.

  • We don't control government processing times. USCIS and consular timelines are outside anyone's control, including ours.
  • We don't guarantee approval. No one legitimately can. We guarantee the speed and quality of our document preparation, not a government agency's decision.
  • We don't handle legal filings. We are not a law firm. Your independent, licensed immigration attorney reviews, files, and represents you.
