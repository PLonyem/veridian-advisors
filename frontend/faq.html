General

Do you guarantee I will get approved?

No. No one can guarantee a government approval. Our service ensures your documents are complete and organized — but final discretion rests with immigration authorities.

Why use you instead of just a law firm?

Law firms focus on legal filing. We focus on the intensive document preparation, financial audit, and source-of-funds verification that causes most delays.

How do I know you are legitimate?

We are a registered private consultancy. We partner with licensed U.S. immigration attorneys and accredited forensic accountants, and we sign a formal engagement letter with every client before any work begins.

Process

Can I do this myself without you?

Yes, but you would need to compile 7 years of tax returns, 5 years of bank records, police clearances from every country you've lived in over the last 10 years, a medical exam, and a provable source of funds. Many clients hire us simply to save the time and avoid costly mistakes.

How long does the whole process take?

Our document preparation takes 4 weeks (Executive) to 6 months (Foundation), depending on your tier. After that, your attorney reviews and files — government processing times vary widely and are outside our control.

What happens after I submit the form?

You'll receive an automated confirmation email. Within 24 hours, a senior advisor will contact you to schedule your confidential consultation.

Pricing

What happens if I am denied?

Our retainer is non-refundable once we begin work — the exception is our Executive tier's 28-day completion guarantee, which is separate from any government decision (see Pricing for details). We pre-vet every prospective client to avoid accepting cases likely to be denied. Government filing fees and legal fees are handled separately by your attorney.

What's the difference between Foundation, Accelerated, and Executive?

Mainly speed and service depth. Foundation (4–6 months) provides basic document coordination. Accelerated (6–8 weeks) adds a dedicated case manager and priority processing. Executive (4 weeks or less, guaranteed) is full white-glove concierge service with a dedicated senior advisor.

Compliance

Can you help if my source of funds is crypto or a complex business structure?

Yes, but you'll need our forensic accountant add-on or the Executive tier, both of which include the deeper documentation this kind of source-of-funds case requires. We will not accept clients whose funds cannot ultimately be verified.

Is my information secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard security practices, and your data is stored in a secure database. We never share your information without your explicit consent, except where required by law — see our Privacy Policy for details.
